ABOUT US

Salem Mavatta Samooga Nala Sangam

4/41 S.K. Township, Salem - 636014
We are proud to inform our Community people, well-wishers, and people in and around Salem. Our Naidu Sangam is more than 50 years old. It has been functioning well all these years, maintaining unity and remark free service. Our motto is "Service Before Self".
Our marriage hall named "Naidu Mahal" is functioning to the need of the people in Salem with all facilities, good ambiance, and moderate rent.
Our marriage bureau is yet another wing of our community welfare scheme, which was established twenty-five years ago. Our Matrimony office is working in a separate building adjoining our Naidu Mahal.
Now we have entered "Online Matrimony" service for the benefits of our Community, for the people in Tamil Nadu in particular and worldwide in general. Thanks to all our Community people for support.
